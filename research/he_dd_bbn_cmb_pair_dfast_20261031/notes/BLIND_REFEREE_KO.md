# Single bounded same-author adverse review: HE-DD physical coeffs

1. **Photons: asymptotic universal-cascade shortcut is unsafe** (supported by Poulin & Serpico PRL 114,091101 (2015), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.114.091101). Model codes here do NOT presume a universal injection spectrum. CMB pair attenuation is necessary, not sufficient for a physical source spectrum. No production promotion.
2. **`Gamma_gg/H` is a local timescale, not a line-of-sight tau**. Any globally sharp cutoff claim would require causal propagation, redshifting of high-E photons, source injection and scattering. Figure names and values label *local redshift crossovers*, not measured photon spectral cutoffs.
3. **Two-channel branching** is a first-event result for only `gamma+He4 -> dd` and `gamma+gamma_CMB -> e+e-`, not a rigorous upper bound on later photons cascaded after pair creation. Another channel could regenerate photons. In particular `P=1` when gamma-gamma is vanishingly rare is not a physical claim that He dd dominates all interactions.
4. **Rice angular/source double counting**. `8.29/4.34 nb` are final published-bin-constrained `P0+P2` integrated fit variants, not measured pointwise `sigma(E)`; no duplicate of 1998 preliminary SAL campaign; 1993 LEGS `npd` contamination not used. The high-bin Schiff `1.546` vs re-integrated `1.2575` and the last Table5.1 error remain CONCERN.
5. **Fast D drag is not BPS**. The electron Maxwellian F(X) with choice of Coulomb logarithm is only controlled as leading logarithm in weakly coupled plasma and for energetic heavy deuterons, with nonrelativistic Maxwellian electrons. The BPS 2005 order-one correction, electron/ion split, nuclear hazards and recoil cannot be claimed. Screening choice is sensitivity bracket, not source uncertainty distribution.
6. **z and thermal assumptions**. Adopted `1e4..2e7` means plasma after helium photofreeze, long before recombination, homogeneous, fully ionized and no e± pair bath. The model is Planck/PDG baseline and NOT BASS-native shear/tetrad/electron state. Neutrino/radiation in H is model adopted. At other epochs, recalculate.
7. **No direct heat**. `dE/dt` electron transfer and `K_d/|dotE|` are neither integrated stopping, instantaneous heat fraction nor `heat_rate`; need energy accounting of all radiative and nuclear daughters. Production `HOLD`.
