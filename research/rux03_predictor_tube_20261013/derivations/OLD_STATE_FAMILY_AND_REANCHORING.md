## 3. Finite change of energy anchor

For a fixed unit timelike anchor \(u_a=\gamma_a(n+\beta_a)\), use the parent's coordinates
\[
a=\mu+B(\zeta),\qquad
t_a=\gamma_a(e-\beta_a\cdot m)-a,\qquad
e=\frac{t_a+a}{\gamma_a}+\beta_a\cdot m.
\]
The lab composition \(\zeta\), momentum \(m\), density \(D\), and time foliation remain the same when the anchor changes. Substitution gives
\[
t_b=\alpha t_a+(\alpha-1)a+
\gamma_b(\boldsymbol\beta_a-\boldsymbol\beta_b)\cdot m,\qquad
\alpha=\gamma_b/\gamma_a. \tag{F10}
\]
The binding \(B\) is affine:
\[
B(\zeta)=b_\chi\cdot\zeta,\quad
b_\chi=(\chi_0,\chi_1,\chi_1+\chi_2).
\]
Thus \(z_b=A_{ba}z_a+c_{ba}\) is affine for each selected anchor pair. \(A_{ba}\) is identity except for its thermal row
\[
\big((\alpha-1)b_\chi^T,\ \alpha,\
 \gamma_b(\beta_a-\beta_b)^T\big),
\]
and \(c_{ba}\) has only thermal component \((\alpha-1)\mu\). The determinant is \(\alpha>0\), so the change is invertible. This is a change of representation of the same lab BE equations, not a boost of the lab integration time.

For general centers \(z_{a,c},z_{b,c}\) and positive scales,
\[
X_b=P X_a+d,\quad
P=H_{s,b}^{-1}A_{ba}H_{s,a},\quad
d=H_{s,b}^{-1}(A_{ba}z_{a,c}+c_{ba}-z_{b,c}). \tag{F11}
\]
The old state transforms to \(X_{{\rm old},b}=P\xi+d\), which must remain in the transformed residual.

## 4. Old-state centering and exact residual equivalence

Choose the new anchor to be the old gas primitive \(u_b(\xi)=u_{\rm gas}(\xi)\), and center the new chart on that same physical old state:
\[
z_{b,c}(\xi)=z_b\bigl(z_{a,c}+H_{s,a}\xi\bigr).
\]
Then the large affine offset cancels exactly:
\[
X_b=P(\xi)(X_a-\xi),\qquad d(\xi)=-P(\xi)\xi. \tag{F12}
\]
This formula should be evaluated on small increments; reconstructing the full rest energy and subtracting two large chart centers discards the precision advantage.

Define
\[
X_a=\xi+P^{-1}X_b,\quad
G_{b,h}(X_b;\xi)=P(\xi)G_h(\xi+P(\xi)^{-1}X_b).
\]
The transformed residual obeys
\[

             =P(\xi)F(X_a,h;\xi). \tag{F13}

Its zero set is exactly the image of the original seven-equation zero set. For a new center that is not the old state, the correct left side is \(X_b-X_{{\rm old},b}-hG_{b,h}\); dropping the transformed old state changes the problem.

At fixed \(\xi\),
\[
J_b=PJP^{-1},\quad J_b^{-1}=PJ^{-1}P^{-1},\quad\det J_b=\det J. \tag{F14}
\]
In the pullback norm \(\|v_b\|_{a,\xi}=\|P^{-1}v_b\|_\infty\), the same contraction factor \(q\) and inverse bound apply exactly. In ordinary infinity norm, valid bounds are multiplied by \(\|P\|_\infty\|P^{-1}\|_\infty\). Axis-aligned enclosures of a transformed box must contain the affine image; they are not silently identified with that image.

## 5. The old-state derivative of the new coordinates

For a family direction \(v\), differentiating (F12) along the root gives
\[

The \(DP\) term is generally nonzero. The source-induced physical increment and the movement of its measuring chart are separate contributions. At \(h=0\), both vanish, hence \(D_\xi X_b(0;\xi)=0\).

\[

## 8. Covariant explanation of the moving-anchor terms

Let the gas stress and fixed-normal energy current be
\[
T^{ab}=(\rho+p)u^a u^b+pg^{ab},\qquad
C^a=-T^{ab}n_b=(\rho+p)\gamma u^a-pn^a.
\]
The anchor projection is
\[
\Psi_b=-u_{b,a}C^a=(\rho+p)\gamma\Gamma_b-p\gamma_b,\qquad
\Gamma_b=-u_b\cdot u.
\]
For independent gas/current and anchor variations,
\[
\delta\Psi_b=-(\delta u_b)\cdot C-u_b\cdot\delta C. \tag{F22}
\]
The first term is required when the old-state anchor changes. At the same old gas state \(u_b=u\), with both varying together by \(\delta u\), normalization gives \(u\cdot\delta u=0\). The added anchor term is \(-p\,\delta\gamma\). It combines with the fixed-anchor result
\[
-u\cdot\delta C=\gamma\,\delta\rho+(\rho+p)\delta\gamma
\]
to give
\[
\delta\Psi_b=\gamma\,\delta\rho+\rho\,\delta\gamma.
\]
At fixed \(D=\gamma n_H\), with \(\rho=n_H\epsilon(a+w)\), this is \(D\epsilon(\delta a+\delta w)\). Therefore
\[
t_b(\text{old})=\Psi_b/(D\epsilon)-a=w,\qquad
\delta t_b(\text{old})=\delta w. \tag{F23}
\]
This total moving-anchor derivative is compatible with the parent's different **fixed-anchor** relation \(\delta t_a=\delta w+(2/3)(w/\gamma)\delta\gamma\).

For two commuting variations, the full mixed current projection is
\[
\delta_1\delta_2\Psi_b
=-(\delta_1\delta_2u_b)\cdot C
-(\delta_1u_b)\cdot\delta_2C
-(\delta_2u_b)\cdot\delta_1C
-u_b\cdot\delta_1\delta_2C. \tag{F24}
\]
Mixed normalization supplies
\(u_b\cdot\delta_1\delta_2u_b=-(\delta_1u_b)\cdot(\delta_2u_b)\).
For a photo current \(\epsilon E_qR(n+e_q)\), a mixed anchor/rate variation retains
\(-\epsilon E_q\,\delta R\,(\delta u_b)\cdot(n+e_q)\).
For escape current \(-\epsilon L u\), it retains
\(\epsilon(\delta u_b)\cdot(\delta L\,u+L\,\delta u)\).
These are the projection terms checked by the new xAct calculation. The species binding subtraction is affine and is then attached to the scalar chart equations above.

The same mixed normalization also controls the moving-anchor spatial projector. Put \(b=u_b\), \(b_i=\delta_i b\), \(b_{12}=\delta_1\delta_2b\), and \(H_b=g+b\otimes b\). Then
\[
(H_b)_{12}
=b_{12}\otimes b+b\otimes b_{12}
 +b_1\otimes b_2+b_2\otimes b_1.
\]
Differentiating \(H_b b=0\) and \(H_b^2=H_b\) twice gives
\[
(H_b)_{12}b+(H_b)_1b_2+(H_b)_2b_1+H_b b_{12}=0,
\]
\[
(H_b)_{12}H_b+(H_b)_1(H_b)_2+(H_b)_2(H_b)_1
 +H_b(H_b)_{12}=(H_b)_{12}.
\]
For a fixed gas current evaluated at \(b=u\), the anchor-curvature term is
\[
-b_{12}\cdot C
=(\rho+p)\gamma\,(b_1\cdot b_2)-p\,\gamma_{b,12},
\qquad \gamma_{b,12}=-n\cdot b_{12}.
\]
All three residuals are included in the new xAct input. The mixed projector calculation uses two independent tangent directions and their constrained mixed derivative.
