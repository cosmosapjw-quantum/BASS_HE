# Finite-mass scientific diagnostic design

## Intended reading

One new scientific figure pair, 180 mm wide × 150 mm high, PNG at 300 dpi and editable-text SVG. Four panels use the same logarithmic temperature axis and consistent species colors: electron blue, neutral H orange and proton green. Labels are in English for mathematical readability. No dual axes are used.

- Panel (a): signed kinetic-energy source per ionization event, Q_s/r in eV. Positive proton heat is a product-birth moment; H removal and electron net energy are instantaneous source terms. Their sum is −13.6 eV per event. This model does not establish species thermal equilibration.
- Panel (b): the small finite-mass electron shift from −I and the combined heavy source, in meV per event. Both use the directly computed small heavy source; a large H-removal/proton-birth subtraction is avoided. This panel resolves a correction hidden on panel (a)'s full scale.
- Panels (c,d): all six species STF response coefficients divided by alpha, with incoming electron and incoming H columns shown separately. The incoming-electron column still requires nH; the incoming-H column requires ne. A signed logarithmic scale with a 2×10^−4 linear neighborhood exposes both damping and birth allocation through zero. These are response coefficients for infinitesimal independent STF perturbations.

The exact constant reduced-mass correction belongs in the metrics/table, not a visually flat plotted line: rate ratio=zeta^(−1/2), electron-only total-STF ratio=sqrt(zeta). The two conditional Jacobi available-energy fractions are retained in CSV/NPZ and representative-row tables. The plot is deliberately reserved for species energy and stress allocation.

## Scope and rendering gate

The source is the declared original LC Jacobi uniform-direction finite-mass model at common T/bulk. The image is rendered only from the completed new saved129-row diagnostic. Rendering invokes no provider/model evaluation. Every render has a unique attempt directory, script copy, input hashes, output hashes and actual CPU/RSS/exit receipt. An independent reviewer will inspect the actual PNG. Any correction is limited to a concrete observed readability or fidelity defect, with the original render retained.
