# Primary source map and exact access

1. B. J. Rice et al., "Two-deuteron photodisintegration of 4He at E_gamma=150–250 MeV", Physical Review C 61, 064612 (2000), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevC.61.064612 (APS: https://journals.aps.org/prc/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevC.61.064612). SOURCE_OBSERVED: Table I 12 absolute DA, source exact JSON byte reproduced. Need original detector response/energy-acceptance as additional owner supplement or thesis full copy, not necessary for fixed-angle kinematic atlas.
2. Bryan Joseph Rice, "The 4He(gamma,dd) Reaction at E_gamma = 150–250 MeV", PhD dissertation Duke University (1998), ProQuest UMI 9825640; TUNL scan https://nucldata.tunl.duke.edu/nucldata/Theses/PhD/Rice_1998.pdf . Supplied PDF stops at 139 pages and does not include Table 5.2. Table 5.1 preliminary values not independent of 2000.
3. R. J. Protheroe, T. Stanev, V. S. Berezinsky, "Electromagnetic cascades and cascade nucleosynthesis in the early Universe", Phys. Rev. D 51 (1995) 4134; DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.51.4134. THEORY_METHOD only: not BASS_HE-specific history.
4. V. Poulin and P. D. Serpico, "Nonuniversal BBN bounds on electromagnetically decaying particles", Phys. Rev. D 91 (2015) 103007; DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.91.103007. THEORY_METHOD: non-universal spectra; own injection source/epoch model required.
5. S. K. Acharya and R. Khatri, "Rich structure of nonthermal relativistic CMB spectral distortions from high energy particle cascades at redshifts z lesssim 2e5", Phys. Rev. D 99 (2019) 043520, DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.99.043520. THEORY_METHOD; not per-fast-deuteron stopping.
6. D. Casas, M. D. Barriga-Carrasco, J. Rubio, "Evaluation of slowing down of proton and deuteron beams in CH2, LiH, and Al partially ionized plasmas", Phys. Rev. E 88 (2013) 033102, DOI 10.1103/PhysRevE.88.033102. STOPPING_METHOD: target composition and energy must match; no transfer to BASS_HE gas without qualification.
7. NIST Standard Reference Database 124, ESTAR/PSTAR/ASTAR, DOI 10.18434/T4NC7P; *only* electrons/protons/helium ions; not a primary deuteron stopping curve. See https://www.nist.gov/pml/stopping-power-range-tables-electrons-protons-and-helium-ions .
8. IAEA PD2019 photonuclear library: https://github.com/IAEA-NDS/IAEA-PD2019 , evaluated total/inclusive yields; exclusive gamma+4He->d+d normalization cannot be inferred from inclusive deuteron yields without channel separation, and energy may end below full Rice 250 MeV window.

Needed external scientific input for complete successor: an actual solver-owned f_gamma(x,p) / injection and EM cascade history covering He-rest 150–250 MeV; n_alpha(x), U_alpha^mu, plasma species/temperature/time; calibrated energy-dependent *exclusive* dd cross-section (Rice detector response and energy-angular acceptance or an independently validated differential model); fast-deuteron Coulomb + nuclear slowing, inelastic cascades, escape and deposition; energy conservation and heat capacity. Until then derived 12-row high-energy atlas is valid only under its printed conditions.
