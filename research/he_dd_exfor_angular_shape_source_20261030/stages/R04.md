Positive raw source contrasts C=(b-a)/(b+a) for each of six angular pairs per observed energy and three energy pairs per observed angle. Competing one-parameter angular candidates y=a_E and y=a_E sin²θ. Competing two-coefficient exchange-even candidate y=sin²θ(a_E+b_E cos²θ) with A_E>0,A_E+B_E>0 on full θ∈[0,π].
