# C3 numerical design

Positive GL quadrature, shared moments/exponentials, scaled ratios and Cauchy checks avoid unstable finite differences and unnecessary dense matrices. A common2x2 loss block advances each infinitesimal STF component. Finite-table tails remain an unresolved physical-input error. Fixed coefficient dissipation and invalid-domain rejection are checked in the scope specified by CODING_CONTRACT.md; no physical positivity repair is introduced.
