## Numerical contracts

- Binary64, explicit SI conversion, no fast-math or implicit mixed precision.
- Positive piecewise-linear sigma with GL16 on each finite bin; the same prepared nodes supply alpha through delta. No tail extrapolation.
- Independent finite-bin SI adaptive integration and GL32 comparison gate2e-9. Actual controls exceed that accuracy within the chosen finite model; source interpolation and missing-tail physical error are unresolved.
- Independent six-dimensional Cartesian controls gate2e-11; vector/scalar full-output parity gate3e-13.
- Positive moments and scaled Cauchy test; signed cross coefficient retained. No artificial positivity repair.
- Explicit physical-zero reason; floating underflow remains distinguishable from physical zero and is rejected by source attachment.
- Positive-density/common-T frozen-coefficient weighted STF norm statement. Anisotropy budget is a caller gate, not a finite-amplitude closure-error bound.
- Charge-neutral heavy/free-electron inventory without positrons or negative ions. The current supplied-event adapter requires all retained particles thermal. A fast-particle population needs a separate number-source provider.
