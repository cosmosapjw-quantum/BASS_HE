# R5 | sharp envelope theorem

For 0<=theta<=90 and coordinate intervals [lo_i,hi_i], existence iff L>=max_(i!=j)(lo_i-hi_j)_+/|theta_i-theta_j|. Extremal f_-=max(0,max_i(lo_i-L|theta-theta_i|)), f_+=min_i(hi_i+L|theta-theta_i|). These are feasible Lipschitz functions and pointwise bound all feasible f. For identical-D symmetric polar law I=4pi int_0^(pi/2) f sin(theta)dtheta. Sharp I bounds are their integrals.
