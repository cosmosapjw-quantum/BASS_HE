## B. Exact kinematic CM->Lab Jacobian
Metric (-,+,+,+). Mass rest energies in MeV: alpha `M=3727.3794118`, deuteron `m=1875.612945`. Photon energy `E` in target at rest, `s=M²+2ME`, `E_* = sqrt(s)/2`, `p_* = sqrt(s/4-m²)` expressed as energy (`p_*c`), `beta=E/(M+E)`, `gamma=(M+E)/sqrt(s)`, `mu=cos(theta_CM)`.

`p_parallel c = gamma[p_*c mu + beta E_*]`, `p_perp c = p_*c sqrt(1-mu²)`, `theta_lab=atan2(p_perp,p_parallel)`.

For axial, one-to-one particle angles, `J=dOmega_lab/dOmega_CM=|dmu_lab/dmu|=gamma (p_*c)^2 |p_*c + beta E_*mu| / [(p_parallel c)²+(p_perp c)²]^(3/2)`; this is the factor multiplying the lab differential cross section to produce CM differential cross section, `dσ_CM/dΩ_CM = J dσ_lab/dΩ_lab`. Bound by the physical forward mapping and fixed E; no detector aperture or finite geometry factor `Q_l` is inferred from this point Jacobian. At original 6 angles each of E170 and E220: all 12 J calculations within 0.00276 of original three-significant-digit printed table; Wolfram independent check passed.
