# FastD stopping in an ionized H/He FLRW plasma: constructive derivation

Beresnyak (ed.), *2023 NRL Plasma Formulary*, "Collisions and Transport", printed p.31, test particle energy relaxation \(\nu^{\alpha|\beta}_\epsilon\), original public NRL PDF: https://www.nrl.navy.mil/Portals/38/PDF%20Files/NRL_Plasma_Formulary_2023.pdf . CEA-R3042 1966 stopping tables in prior PR129 are **neutral target matter**, not fully ionized primordial plasma. The ascribed 1965 source-born 4 D energies in this unit come from Asbury/Loeffler source-conditioned reconstructed kinematics as used by PR129, **not individual experimentally measured kinetic values**.

Signature (-,+,+,+), nonrel \(K_D=\tfrac12m_Dv^2\), \(k_BT(z)=k_BT_0(1+z)\), deuteron \(Z_D=+1\), target species \(b\in\{e^-,p,^4\mathrm{He}^{++}\}\). \(c,\hbar,k_B\) are retained explicitly. Set \(n_b(z)=n_{b0}(1+z)^3\), \(n_e=(1-Y_P/2)n_b\), \(n_p=(1-Y_P)n_b\), \(n_\alpha=Y_P n_b/4\). Fully ionized all species at equal \(T=T_\gamma\) is a *specified source-conditional plasma background*, not derived from BASS dynamical chemistry. Instantaneous background approximation is checked through \(t_\mathrm{char}H\).

**NRL Rosenbluth test-particle energy moment**, Gaussian units at original source, translated to SI using \(e^2\mapsto e^2/(4\pi\epsilon_0)\):

\[\psi(x)=\mathrm{erf}(\sqrt{x})-\frac{2\sqrt{x}}{\sqrt\pi}e^{-x},\quad \psi'(x)=\frac{2\sqrt{x}}{\sqrt\pi}e^{-x},\quad x_b=\frac{m_bv_D^2}{2k_BT}.\]
\[\nu_{0b}=\frac{4\pi n_b(Z_DZ_b)^2(e^2/(4\pi\epsilon_0))^2\ln\Lambda_b}{m_D^2v_D^3},\quad \nu_{\epsilon,b}=2\nu_{0b}\bigg[\frac{m_D}{m_b}\psi(x_b)-\psi'(x_b)\bigg].\]
\[\dot K_b=-K_D\nu_{\epsilon,b},\qquad \dot K_{\rm tot}=\sum_b\dot K_b.\]

The NRL \(\nu_\epsilon\) is defined by the **ensemble moment** \(d\langle v^2\rangle/dt=-\nu_\epsilon v^2\), not an exact stochastic single-ion deterministic trajectory. Modeling a characteristic narrow beam with \(\dot K=-\sum_bK\nu_{\epsilon,b}\) is an explicitly conditional closure when \(K\gg k_BT\). To avoid identifying low-energy diffusion with deterministic friction, terminate each trajectory at \(K_\mathrm{end}=\max(1\,\mathrm{keV},30k_BT)\). This terminal kinetic energy is **untracked** and must not be reclassified as heat.

Debye screening adopts \(\lambda_D^{-2}=e^2( n_e+n_p+4n_\alpha)/(\epsilon_0 k_BT)\). Characteristic minimum impact parameter \(b_\mathrm{min}=\max( Z_DZ_b e^2/(4\pi\epsilon_0\mu_bv_{\mathrm{rel}}^2),\hbar/(2\mu_bv_{\mathrm{rel}}))\) with \(v_{\mathrm{rel}}^2=v_D^2+3k_BT/m_b\) and \(\mu_b=m_Dm_b/(m_D+m_b)\); \(\ln\Lambda_b=\ln(\lambda_D/b_\mathrm{min})\). The exact Coulomb logarithm is not known at leading-log order: an alternate **fixed** \(\ln\Lambda=20\) is reported as a physical model sensitivity, not quadrature error. This Debye approximation ignores dynamical dielectric screening and BPS next-order stopping.

**Computational physical Green response** for input \(K_0\), fixed \(z\):
\[ t_\mathrm{char}(K_0,z)=\int_{K_\mathrm{end}}^{K_0}\frac{dK}{S_\mathrm{tot}(K,z)},\quad \mathcal E_b=\int_{K_\mathrm{end}}^{K_0}\frac{S_b(K,z)}{S_\mathrm{tot}(K,z)}dK,\quad S_b=-\dot K_b.\]
\[L_\mathrm{char}=\int_{K_\mathrm{end}}^{K_0}\frac{v_D(K)dK}{S_\mathrm{tot}},\qquad \mathcal N_t=\int n_t(z)v_D\,dt=n_t(z)L_\mathrm{char}\quad (t=p,\alpha).\]

Since our background is frozen, \(\mathcal N_t\) is an *exposure / target column*, independent of specific nuclear reaction cross sections. \(\tau^{\rm diagnostic}=\mathcal N_t\,(1\mathrm{mb})\) is a scale diagnostic, not the physical cross section. Actual reaction probability requires \(\int dK\,n_tv\sigma_r(K)/S_\mathrm{tot}\) with published \(\sigma_r\) and any angular/secondary processes. This step is a natural next experiment, not a missing experimental-source blocker. The available collision model is a high-energy nonrel approximation; tagged \(K_0>30\) MeV is *extrapolative* (e.g. 150 MeV corresponds to \(K/(m_Dc^2)\simeq0.08\)). Use the exact relativistic velocity diagnostic only for warning, not silently with nonrel NRL energy moments.

**Physical interpretation**: \(\mathcal E_e+\mathcal E_p+\mathcal E_\alpha=K_0-K_\mathrm{end}\) and \(tH\) usually is small in this model. Gross collision-mediated energy transfer is not the final thermal deposited energy: reprocessed secondaries, redshift, collective response, nuclear in-flight reactions and terminal \(K_\mathrm{end}\) remain unresolved. No production \(\dot q\), \(\kappa\), \(R_\mathrm{event}\), or \(L4\) is introduced.
