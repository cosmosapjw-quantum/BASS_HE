Wolfram symbolic checks for R5

1. For generic complex 2x2 J=A+iB and realification JR={{A,-B},{B,A}}:
Det(JR) - (Re Det(J))^2 - (Im Det(J))^2 -> 0
Therefore Det(JR)=|Det(J)|^2.

2. For J={{a,b},{c,d}} and differential dJ:
d(det J) - Tr(adj(J).dJ) -> 0.

3. In adapted rank-one fold coordinates, for H=(F1,F2,det DzF),
DH={{a,0,m},{0,0,alpha},{q,a beta,r}}
and Wolfram gives
Det(DH) = -a^2 alpha beta.

These are algebraic sanity checks, not an interval certificate for BASS_HE.
