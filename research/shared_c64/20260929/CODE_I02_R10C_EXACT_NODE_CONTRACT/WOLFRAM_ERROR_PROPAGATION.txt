R10C fresh Wolfram checks

For p(Delta)=exp[-f Delta/v], f>0, v>0:

1. p(Dq)/p(Dr) = exp[f(Dr-Dq)/v].
2. dp/dDelta = -(f/v) exp[-f Delta/v].
3. Reversible crossing block T(p)=[[1-p,p],[p,1-p]]:
   ||T(q)-T(p)||_1 = 2 |q-p|.
4. Absorbing block A(p)=[[1-p,0],[p,1]]:
   ||A(q)-A(p)||_1 = 2 |q-p|.
5. For Eq.(50), with the crossing sequence on both sides of an unchanged
   column-stochastic rotation, product telescoping gives the conservative nodewise
   state bound
   ||delta y||_1 <= 4 Sum_e |delta p_e|.
6. If |delta Delta|<=eps:
   |p(Delta+delta)/p(Delta)-1| <= exp[f eps/v]-1.

These are algebraic perturbation bounds only. They do not certify the continuum
rho integral, contour-panel error, or physical model.
