# Source-owned experimental kernels: mathematical derivation

Assume reference FLRW with tetrad comoving observers, metric (-,+,+,+), fully ionized H/He, massless injected gamma. The lab-reported target-rest cross section of an EXFOR source is a physical observable at *the reported photon energy only*, and its nuclear reaction and quantity flags determine any legal transport mapping. `M0475003` is He4(g,d)d exclusive total in nb; `M0484003` is HE4(g,p)T exclusive total in microbarn; `M0520002` is photoabsorption inclusive total in microbarn, no exclusive nuclear product; `G4081002` is a polarization asymmetry, no cross-section units; `L0138002` is neutral pion production, not npD.

At the exact incident measured energy E and reference z, the partially measured local first-interaction hazard rate is `Gamma_j = n_alpha(z) c sigma_j(E)` (s^-1), with `sigma_j(E)` source measured and all other source-model rates frozen. For the dimensionless channel fraction `p_j=Gamma_j/Gamma_sum`, `dp_j/dsigma_j=p_j(1-p_j)/sigma_j` with other channel rates fixed. Separate reported statistical and systematic components. Correlated 5% pT systematic across energies never counts as independent evidence per point. For DD only statistical error is reported, therefore the unreported systematic is UNKNOWN rather than zero.

Since Maccormick(1997) measured the *inclusive* photoabsorption, a distinct physically defensible denominator is `Gamma_sum^incl=Gamma_BW+Gamma_KN+Gamma_BH+n_alpha c sigma_abs`. Its measured exclusive channel share obeys `sigma_j/sigma_abs`, but sigma_abs is interpolated from neighboring measured energies E_a,E_b and is not an observation at the energy E itself. `sigma_abs(E)= (1-w)sigma_abs(E_a)+w sigma_abs(E_b)`, `w=(E-E_a)/(E_b-E_a)`. For independent reported statistical errors `s_abs^2=(1-w)^2s_a^2+w^2s_b^2`. For fully correlated same-source systematic error `s_sys(abs)=(1-w)s_sys,a+w s_sys,b`. Cross-experiment covariance is unknown and independence is a diagnostic only. `p_j^incl=n_alpha c sigma_j/Gamma_sum^incl`; holding independent measured sigma_abs fixed, `dp/dsigma_j=p/sigma_j`, `dp/dsigma_abs=-p(n_alpha c/Gamma_sum^incl)`. No covariance-based absolute confidence claim.

For the 1958 75-170 MeV energy-integrated cross section `I_c=integral_75^170 sigma_c(E) dE`, use the independent Cyburt(2003) source-functional form `sigma_c(E)=A_c [(Q_c/E)^a (1-Q_c/E)^b] mb` when E>Q_c. The integrand has dimensions mb and the integral mb MeV. Direct quadrature yields `I_npD^model=2.80926051844` and `I_pT+nHe3^model=7.64764673278`, compared to Table I source values 4.6±1.2 and 10.0±1.9 *stat only*. Neither inversion to a 170-MeV point measurement nor independent summation of the `<75`, `75-170`, `<170` source intervals is allowed.

A key new quantitative **model-completeness** diagnostic is `g(E)=1-[sigma_pT+sigma_nHe3+sigma_npD+sigma_dd]/sigma_abs^interp`; one exclusive channel may be set to its measured value while others remain model. At source-matched 223/225/239 MeV this is about 0.990. This is a diagnostic of missing source-absorbing channels conditional on non-measured model components, not an inferred individual measured reaction or a full coupled electromagnetic cascade. BW/BH electron/positron return, photon gamma-gamma redistribution, and prompt/secondary pion/nucleon transport remain outside the model. No full cosmic event rate, D energy deposition or heat can be inferred.
